About Med Tech

A polished medical platform built as one consistent product.

The project brings together doctor discovery, schedule-based booking, AI support, and Telegram workflows inside one consistent experience.

User flows Patient · doctor · admin
AI service OpenAI · Grok · Gemini
Booking Slots and doctor schedule

What the platform includes

The product already demonstrates the main user flow: registration, email confirmation, dashboard, doctors map, appointments, AI chat, and Telegram connection.

Doctors and clinics map with live markers
Booking by days, working hours, and free slots
Different UI and permissions for patient, doctor, and admin
AI assistant with saved conversation history and provider fallback
Telegram bot for fast actions and reminders

Why this project exists

Med Tech was designed to feel like a finished product instead of a collection of disconnected pages.

That means the backend flow matters, but the presentation, UX, and visual consistency matter just as much.

Stack and integrations

Django, CBV, Forms, Filters, Signals, Soft delete
Custom templates, static files, responsive UI
SMTP email confirmation and Telegram integration
AI service with history stored in AIChatSession
